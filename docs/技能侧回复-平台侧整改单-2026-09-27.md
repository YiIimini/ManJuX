## 5. D5（建议）`retention_analysis` 统一成官方形态

两处（派生 `H3-提示词.md` 与生成器 `manju_plan.py`）现在都是：

```text
retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 1]): fully_preserved - the same face, hairstyle, costume and signature detail are retained exactly as on that character's front sheet.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1]): partially_preserved - the location's layout, landmarks, materials and lighting are retained from the scene plate, while the action and the camera move over it.
```

单镜提示词 ⇒ 只可能出现在 `[Shot 1]`，所以 `(appears in [Shot 1])` 是准确的；
`<Subject N>` 标签按官方样例（不再是 `Picture N — 名`），标记词保持官方那几个 ✓。
