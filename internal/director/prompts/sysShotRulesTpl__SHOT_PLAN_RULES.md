## SHOT PLAN RULES
- **DIALOGUE IS MANDATORY AND VERBATIM (2026-09-29 user: 「分镜配音严重大问题啊，完全听不清说的啥」)**: every line that is spoken in the shot goes into `dialogue` as `{"speaker": "…", "text": "…"}` with the **words written out in full Chinese** — `text` may never be empty. A line with a speaker and no text is worse than no line at all: the renderer then has nothing to say, the model improvises its own audio, and the audience hears gibberish that sounds like Chinese but is not a sentence (measured: ASR of one such clip came back as 「明筹者任劝行表档化﹔我们补偿﹔好,行物则顺左。」). The same words must also appear inside `<d>[Chinese]…</d>` in the `h3_prompt`, in the same order. If a shot has no speech, leave `dialogue` empty — do not invent a speaker.
- **FIELDS THE PLATFORM ACTUALLY READS (2026-09-29 audit: the schema never asked for them, so the model silently left them empty — same shape as the empty-`dialogue` bug)**: fill these on **every** shot —
  - `positions`: where each character is in the frame (official Character Positions sub-field; the pre-flight gate requires it for every character in the shot). Example: `{"碑生（丙十七）": "画面左侧、中景、面朝右"}`.
  - `vo_kind`: `speech` (someone on screen is speaking) / `inner` (inner monologue performed by the Q-version figure) / `system` (system voice + panel) / `narration` (voice-over). This decides which reference images the shot gets, and whether the "lips stay closed" clause is added — a wrong value makes the wrong person mouth the words.
  - `narration`: the voice-over text, verbatim; empty string when there is none.
  - `chibi_ids`: the Q-version card ids used by an `inner` shot; empty array otherwise.
  - `end_state`: what is visible at the END of the clip (the state the next clip continues from) — only things you can see (positions, posture, props in hand), never mood or sound.
- **SCENE QUOTA (2026-09-30 user: 「分析是否场景过于单一」/「好好完善」)**: the episode brief you are given lists its scenes as `【场 N|地点|时间|内外】`. Allocate shots **by those scenes, in that order**: every scene in the brief gets at least one shot, and **no single scene may take more than 40% of the episode's shots** — measured failure: one episode put 7 of 15 shots (49%, 63 s) on a single slope, cut the third scene down to one 5-second shot and dropped the night scene entirely, so 82% of the episode sat in one outdoor daylight location and the audience called it 「场景过于单一」. When the brief covers several time-of-day / interior-exterior states, **cover them**: the night scene and the interior scenes are where the tonal and rhythmic variation of the episode comes from. If a scene carries more story than the others, vary it from *inside* (different camera angles and times within the same place) instead of spending more shots on it.
- **CAMERA VARIETY (2026-10-09 user: 「分镜运镜很单一」)**: the official camera-motion vocabulary (`base-en.txt` §4.3) is exactly 12 types — Zoom / Push / Pan / Truck / Tilt / Pedestal / Arc / Tracking / Static / Shake / POV / Roll. Across one episode use **at least four different types**, and never run the whole episode on the same one or two — measured complaint: every clip is a push-in or a pull-back, so the episode reads as one long camera move no matter how the shot sizes change. Pair the motion with the beat: push in for a reveal, pan to hand attention to someone, tracking to follow a walk, static when the line or the face is the whole point. Write it as a natural English action (`The camera pushes in …`, one motion per shot unless a compound move is genuinely meant — `panning right while pushing in`), and never write camera jargon (dolly / crane / steadicam).
- **AT MOST TWO LINES PER CLIP, EACH INSIDE ITS OWN INNER SHOT (2026-09-29 user: 「配音存在重复的」)**: measured — one 9-second clip carrying **four** lines came back with one of them spoken **twice** (ASR: 「…碑是你自己刻的, 碑是你自己刻的, 碑号是营里烙的。」). The renderer places each line at a cut, and when the lines outnumber the beats it repeats one. So: **at most two spoken lines per clip**; a beat with more dialogue must be split into more clips. Put each line's `<d>[Chinese]…</d>` **inside the inner-shot block where it is spoken** (`[Shot 2] At 00:03.500 … <d>…</d>`), never all of them bunched at the start — a tag outside a cut has no place on the timeline and gets read out again.
- **CLIP LENGTH RULE (user's own storyboard spec, 2026-09-28)**: a non-final clip is **7, 8, 9 or 10 seconds** —
  pick whichever ends on a natural stop (a person settles, a turn completes, a gaze lands, a line finishes).
  The FINAL clip takes whatever remains of the episode. So the **clip count follows from the episode length**:
  roughly "episode seconds / 8.5", not "one clip per story beat". Measured before this rule: 35 clips at an
  average of 6.8 s (and 55 on an earlier episode) — every extra clip is one more GPU generation, one more
  seam, one more chance to drift, and the episode feels choppy instead of tight.
- **USE THE SCRIPT'S STRUCTURE (2026-09-29 user: 「内容转剧本不给力，导致分镜脚本不给力」)**: the episode brief you are given is a structured script — every scene carries 目的 / 冲突 / 转折 / 爽点 / 钩子, plus an 【开场爆点】 line and a 【关键道具与信息】 list. **Read those fields and let them drive the shot list**: the 爽点 is where you spend the close-ups and the longest shot, the 转折 is where the angle changes, the 目的 decides what the scene must show. Never distribute coverage evenly across beats — that is exactly what makes an episode feel flat. Every 道具 in 【关键道具与信息】 must appear with the position stated there (e.g. 「蹲在碑生左肩上」 means the figure sits on that shoulder in frame).
- **VIEW DISCIPLINE (`view` / `view_note`)**: the official `view` strings are a **big-camera table** (aerial / eye-level deep perspective / compressed depth / conditional low-or-peek angles / telephoto) — a plain 平视中近景 or 俯拍特写 has no entry there, and **inventing a wrong string is worse than leaving it blank** (it drags the picture off). So: use one of the nine verbatim when it genuinely fits; when it does not fit, **leave `view` empty and write `view_note` saying why** (e.g. 「平视中近景，官方表无对应项」「主观镜头」「越轴反打」). Blank view **without** a note is the one thing that is not allowed — 2026-09-29 实测那一集 11 镜里 8 镜既没 view 也没说明，成片机位乱跳、越轴，用户的原话是「前后镜漂移，压不住场」。Also: consecutive shots in the same scene stay on the **same side of the axis**; crossing it deliberately must be written in the action (and in `view_note`).
- Within each clip, split into **2-4 internal shots of 1.5-4 s** (see 抓重点纪律 §10). Length lives at the
  **clip** level; rhythm lives at the **internal cut** level. Do not confuse the two: many short clips is slow,
  few long clips with internal cuts is fast. If the source has many beats, use more shots — a dense chapter may legitimately need 15-30 shots, and that is fine. Always prefer MORE, smaller shots over fewer overloaded ones; never compress several actions into one shot to save shot count. Each shot {{MIN}}-{{MAX}} seconds (integer): local H3 rendering costs grow faster than linear with frame count (a 10s shot is ~2.8x the load of a 6s one on the same card), so split a long beat into two shots instead of stretching one. Hook in shot 1, payoff at the end.
- CONTINUITY (critical, 2026-09-10): consecutive shots must read as ONE continuous flow. Each shot's action must BEGIN where the previous shot ENDED — same location (unless a deliberate scene change), carried-over posture/emotion/props, coherent time-of-day and lighting. NEVER skip a narrative beat the audience needs; if a beat needs two camera moments, split it into two shots with one clear action each. On scene changes, bridge with narration or a matching visual anchor, never jump-cut blind.
- shot_size 景别用中文:远景/全景/中景/近景/特写。Emotional beats and dialogue MUST use 近景/特写 (H3 face tokens are scarce; closer framing beats wider framing).
- NO CARD, NO FACE (critical, 2026-09-27): 特写/近景 puts a face on screen, and a face can only come from a reference image — those are attached from THIS SHOT's characters list, nothing else. So before writing 特写/近景, make sure every person whose face is visible is listed in characters (bound to their approved card). If a person has no card (or you deliberately do not want to fix their identity), then do NOT frame them close: use a back view, a partial body, or an anonymous crowd — AND DO NOT describe their face at all. When a shot genuinely needs a person on screen with no card, keep the framing off their face AND mark it explicitly with "unbound": true plus "unbound_reason": "no_card" (the person is not in this episode's cast) or "faceless_by_design" (you chose to keep them faceless) — that flag is what tells the contract check this is deliberate, not a forgotten binding. Never pair a close framing with an anchor that denies the person ("empty frame, no people") while the action describes a face: that contradiction makes the model paint a brand-new stranger's face on every take, and the audience sees a different person each time.
- One camera move per shot（**平台规则**：官方 §4.3 只规定"运动类型＋幅度＋速度"三件套，
  并**没有**说每镜必须有一个运镜 —— 这条是我们为了"一镜到底感"加的，别当官方口径引用）,
  written as a **natural English action inside the shot** (never stacked as labels at the end of a
  sentence), from the official motion vocabulary ONLY: Zoom In / Zoom Out (focal length changes, camera body
  still), Push In / Pull Out (camera moves forward / backward), Pan Left / Pan Right (lens pivots horizontally), Truck Left / Truck Right (camera translates sideways), Tilt Up / Tilt Down (lens pivots vertically), Pedestal Up / Pedestal Down (whole camera rises / descends), Arc Shot (arcs around the subject), Tracking Shot (follows a moving subject), Static Shot (camera and lens still), Shake Slightly / Shake Strongly, POV, Roll Clockwise / Roll Counterclockwise. Add amplitude ("with small amplitude" / "with large amplitude") and speed ("at slow speed" / "at fast speed") ONLY when they carry meaning — the official guide omits medium amplitude and normal speed. NEVER use jargon like dolly, crane, whip pan（**平台规则**：官方只给词表、没有明说禁术语；加这条是因为 H3 认的是自然动作，术语进正文只是噪音）.
  **TIME-RAMP (slow motion) — 平台规则，官方词表里没有**：用户的《五》点名要"慢动作"，
  而官方 §4.3 的 12 种运动类型里**没有它** —— 官方给的 `at slow speed` 是**运镜**走得慢，
  与"**动作被放慢**"不是一回事。要时间放慢时写 `in slow motion`（并把这一镜写得比常规长一点，
  让放慢后的动作装得下）；**它是平台规则、不是官方口径**，效果以实测为准，别冒充官方引用。
- CUTS have their own vocabulary (official §4.2): "the camera cuts to" / "the shot cuts to" / "the shot transitions to" / "the shot changes to" / "the shot switches to". A cut must introduce NEW information — subject, space, state, viewpoint or time; if only the distance or a slight angle needs to change, prefer CAMERA MOTION over a cut. Cross-dissolve / fade / wipe only where they are deliberately asked for (our between-scene rule asks for a fade — see the continuity section).
- mode: "ref2va" when character/scene reference images will be provided (any shot with on-screen characters); "t2va" for pure text-to-video shots without references.
- dialogue speaker must match a character name, or use speaker "旁白" for narration. Keep spoken lines short (≤20 chars), punchy, ending with 。?! naturally. At most 2 speakers per shot.
- DIALOGUE MUST FIT THE SHOT (critical): Mandarin delivery runs about {{CPS}} characters per second, and H3 needs roughly one extra second of headroom to start and settle. The shot's duration MUST be at least ceil(total_dialogue_chars / {{CPS}}) + 1 (rounded up to a whole second, min {{MIN}}, max {{MAX}}). A 20-character line therefore needs 6 seconds — never park a 20-character line in a {{MIN}}-second shot: the speech gets cut off and the finished video loses the end of the line. If a line cannot fit {{MAX}} seconds, split the beat into two shots, each carrying part of the line.
